## Assessment gates

- Diagnostic: 24 mixed items to establish a starting point.
- Module checkpoints: nine items after each of six modules.
- Cumulative review: 60 mixed items with rationales.
- Readiness forms A and B: 120 items, 150 minutes, separately scored market/regulation sections.
- Workspace readiness: 80% overall and at least 75% in each section. Official pass standard: 70% in each part.

## Simplification policy

Every worked product uses stated fictional specifications. Actual contract units, ticks, delivery rules, limits, settlement, order availability, and margin must be checked against current exchange/FCM rules. Regulatory definitions can contain exclusions and exemptions; the current rule text controls.
