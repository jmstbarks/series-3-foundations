# Mission: Series 3

## Why

Pass the Series 3 examination while building a durable mental model of futures, options on futures, hedging, market mechanics, and the regulatory responsibilities that protect customers.

## Success looks like

- Explain the economic purpose and lifecycle of a futures contract without relying on memorized jargon.
- Calculate ticks, futures profit or loss, margin equity, basis, hedge results, and option payoffs accurately.
- Choose appropriate hedges, orders, and basic futures or option positions from a described risk.
- Distinguish the principal registrant roles and recognize core customer-protection, disclosure, promotional, and supervisory duties.
- Score at least 80% overall on a full practice exam, with neither market knowledge nor regulations below 75%.

## Constraints

- Beginner starting point: no derivatives knowledge is assumed.
- Typical study session: 45–60 minutes over approximately 4–8 weeks.
- Claims must be supported by current official NFA, CFTC, FINRA, or exchange education sources.
- The Series 3 requires a passing score on both market knowledge and regulations; strength in one part cannot compensate for failure in the other.

## Out of scope

- Recommendations to place real trades or risk capital.
- Proprietary question banks or copied commercial prep materials.
- Contract-specific trading rules presented as timeless facts.
- Building or deploying the low-poly simulation before the foundation passes review.
