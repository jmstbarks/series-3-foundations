# Teaching Notes

- Learner preference: pass and retain, not short-term memorization alone.
- Starting level: new to derivatives.
- Pacing: one 20–25 minute lesson plus retrieval practice and review in a 45–60 minute session.
- Use `learning-records/` only after the learner demonstrates knowledge, corrects a misconception, or discloses meaningful prior knowledge. Do not create records for mere lesson completion.
- Target readiness: 80% overall, market knowledge at least 75%, and regulations at least 75%.
- The official passing standard remains 70% on each part; the higher workspace thresholds are a study buffer.
- Regulatory material was retrieved on 2026-08-14 and should be refreshed near the exam date.
