# Series 3 Resources

Sources last checked: **2026-08-14**. Regulatory and exam-administration details should be rechecked near the examination date.

## Knowledge

- [NFA: Study Outline for Futures Industry Exams](https://www.nfa.futures.org/registration-membership/study-outlines/index.html)
  The curriculum coverage contract. Use for: every Series 3 topic, exam procedures, and the distinction between market knowledge and U.S. regulations.
- [FINRA: Series 3 — National Commodities Futures Exam](https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series3)
  Current exam administrator summary. Use for: scored-question count, duration, test delivery, and the requirement to earn 70% on each part.
- [NFA: Proficiency Requirements](https://www.nfa.futures.org/registration-membership/how-to-register/proficiency-requirements.html)
  Official registration and proficiency context. Use for: who generally needs Series 3 and the fact that passing an exam alone is not registration.
- [CFTC: Futures Market Basics](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/EducationCenter/FuturesMarketBasics/index2.htm)
  Regulator-authored primer. Use for: contracts, hedgers and speculators, margin, daily marking to market, customer risk, and registration context.
- [CFTC: Economic Purpose of Futures Markets and How They Work](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/economicpurpose.html)
  Detailed official explanation. Use for: hedging, clearing, offset, convergence, delivery, and margin examples.
- [CFTC: Futures Glossary](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/CFTCGlossary/index.htm)
  Primary terminology reference. Use for: definitions in the workspace glossary and for resolving ambiguous market vocabulary.
- [CFTC: Speculative Position Limits](https://www.cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/MarketSurveillance/SpeculativeLimits/speculativelimits.html)
  Official overview of limits, aggregation, and hedge exemptions. Use for: the purpose and broad structure of position limits.
- [NFA: Rulebook](https://www.nfa.futures.org/rulebooksql/rules.aspx)
  Current NFA requirements and interpretive notices. Use for: Rules 2-4, 2-9, 2-13, 2-29, 2-30, registration, discipline, and arbitration.
- [NFA: Regulatory Requirements for FCMs, IBs, CPOs and CTAs](https://www.nfa.futures.org/members/member-resources/files/regulatory-requirements-guide.pdf)
  NFA compliance guide. Use for: registrant duties, supervision, recordkeeping, customer funds, and required disclosures.
- [NFA: Communications with the Public Guide](https://www.nfa.futures.org/members/member-resources/files/promo-material-guide.pdf)
  NFA promotional-material guide. Use for: misleading claims, high-pressure practices, performance presentations, review, and recordkeeping.
- [CME Institute: Introduction to Futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-futures)
  Exchange-authored course. Use for: contract specifications, tick movements, notional value, expiration, settlement, margin, hedgers, and speculators.
- [CME Institute: Introduction to Options](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/cme-institute-live/chapter-1-introduction-to-cme-group-and-fundamentals-of-financial-futures-and-options/introduction-to-options)
  Exchange-authored introduction. Use for: options on futures, premiums, exercise, buyer and writer obligations, and payoff structure.
- [CME Institute: Option Strategies](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/option-strategies)
  Exchange-authored strategy course. Use for: protective options, vertical spreads, risk, and payoff comparisons.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [NFA BASIC](https://www.nfa.futures.org/basicnet/)
  Official registration and disciplinary-history lookup. Use for: testing whether a firm or individual is properly registered and for seeing regulation applied in practice.
- [CFTC Customer Protection](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/index.htm)
  Regulator education and advisories. Use for: current fraud patterns, customer rights, and the practical consequences of leverage and misconduct.

## Gaps

- Official sources define tested subjects but do not publish a complete official practice-question bank. Workspace questions are original teaching items, not recalled or represented exam questions.
- Product specifications, margin levels, order availability, limits, and settlement procedures vary by contract and can change. Worked examples therefore state all needed assumptions and are labeled as simplified.
- The two practice forms use a study allocation of 85 market-knowledge and 35 regulation questions. This mirrors a common preparation convention but is not presented as a current official section weighting because the public FINRA and NFA pages specify only the 120 scored-question total and two-part passing rule.
