Position and payoff map

Turn a market view or cash exposure into the correct direction, right, or obligation.

Futures

Long futures

Bullish. Gains as futures rise; loses as they fall. Symmetric obligation.

P/L = exit − entry
Futures

Short futures

Bearish. Gains as futures fall; loses as they rise. Symmetric obligation.

P/L = entry − exit
Option buyer

Long call

Bullish right to buy. Max loss premium; BE strike + premium.

max(0,F−K)−P
Option buyer

Long put

Bearish right to sell. Max loss premium; BE strike − premium.

max(0,K−F)−P
Option writer

Short call

Receives premium; assigned to the selling side if exercised. Uncovered upside risk can be unlimited.

P−max(0,F−K)
Option writer

Short put

Receives premium; assigned to the buying side if exercised. Downside risk can be very large.

P−max(0,K−F)
