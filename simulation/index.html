What this teaches

This is a spatial walkthrough of one commercial futures hedge: cash exposure → hedge choice → order → exchange match → clearing → daily settlement → basis → offset → compliance review. The cart carries the actual contract count, its color marks long or short, and its P/L appears only after settlement.

Fidelity ledger

Computed: hedge side; rounded contract count; hedged units and ratio; tick value; entry with directional slippage; notional; cash and futures prices; basis change; futures P/L; daily variation margin; maintenance breaches and restore-to-initial calls; effective cash price; and four explicit compliance checks.

Scaled: one visible crate per contract, capped at ten; price changes and daily settlements are compressed into a short trip; the side gauge shows station progress.

Assumed: a fictional 5,000-unit contract with a $0.0025 tick; starting cash $5.10 and futures $5.20; five settlement fractions; maintenance at 78% of the selected initial margin; immediate fills; customer funds at an FCM; and an illustrative 100-contract position limit.

Faked or omitted: order-book mechanics, liquidity and partial fills; exchange-specific contract and margin rules; clearing default resources; delivery grades, locations and notices; tax/accounting effects; and the scenery. This is exam education, not trading advice or a compliance system.

Pacing and controls

First visits stop for 9–26 seconds based on reading length; repeat runs are quick. Space plays or pauses, S steps, R replays the slow tour, F toggles follow, L toggles labels, and Escape closes this dialog. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and double-click or use ⤢ to fit the town.
